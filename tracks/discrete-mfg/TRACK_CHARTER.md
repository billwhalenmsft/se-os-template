# Discrete manufacturing track charter

## Purpose

Provide reusable, customer-free patterns for manufacturers with assets,
production operations, service teams, channel partners, and quality processes.

## Seed motions

- Predictive-maintenance framing
- Field-service dispatch recommendations
- Warranty and returns triage
- Case routing and knowledge assistance
- Supplier or distributor exception handling

## Quality gates

- Synthetic examples in the public template
- Clear human review before operational writes
- Outcome and baseline defined before build
- Reset and fallback documented for every demo
