# AI Business Process track charter

## Mission

Frame and prove AI-assisted business-process improvements with measurable
outcomes, visible evidence, and human decision points.

## Verticals

- Discrete manufacturing
- Process manufacturing
- Mobility
- Wholesale distribution

## Motions

- Customer service
- Field service
- Sales
- Finance and operations
- Power Platform workflow modernization

## Rules

- Use synthetic examples in the public template.
- Define the outcome and baseline before building.
- Keep operational writes behind explicit review-and-go approval.
- Harvest only reviewed, minimized patterns.
