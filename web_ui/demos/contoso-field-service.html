Contoso field-service demo pattern

Fictional organization and synthetic scenario

1. Stakes

Dispatch delays create missed arrival windows and avoidable travel.

2. Signal

A new work order arrives with priority, location, skill, and parts requirements.

3. Decision

The experience recommends a technician and explains availability, skills, and route fit.

4. Action

The dispatcher reviews the recommendation before any schedule write.

5. Outcome

Measure median dispatch time, on-time arrival, and manual reassignment rate.
