# How My SE-OS relates to MCAPS OS

## My SE-OS

A generated repository is the operator's private experiment and delivery
environment. It may contain local configuration and authorized customer work,
but those materials stay in that private instance.

## MCAPS OS

MCAPS OS is the broader shared contract and orchestration layer. It can provide
reviewed patterns, role-aware experiences, and cross-team coordination without
requiring every private workspace to centralize its raw context.

## What may be shared

- reviewed, reusable patterns;
- minimized schemas and contracts;
- synthetic examples;
- provenance and version metadata;
- validation evidence.

## What remains private

- customer notes and records;
- account assignments and strategy;
- Microsoft 365 messages, meetings, and transcripts;
- environment URLs and identifiers;
- credentials and connector responses;
- unreviewed drafts and hypotheses.

## Onboarding

Generating this template does not join an MCAPS OS network. Registration,
shared providers, automation schedules, and authority grants require a separate,
approved onboarding process.
