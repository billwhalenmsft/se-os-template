# Synthetic motion matrix

Use these fictional patterns to choose a demo shape.

| Motion | Fictional pattern | Core proof | Primary measure |
|---|---|---|---|
| Customer service | Fabrikam grounded answer | Cite approved knowledge, then require agent review | Handling time and correction rate |
| Field service | Contoso dispatch recommendation | Explain technician fit before scheduling | Dispatch time and reassignment rate |
| Sales | Northwind opportunity brief | Summarize synthetic account and pipeline facts | Prep time and factual accuracy |
| Operations | Adventure Works exception triage | Rank synthetic exceptions with evidence | Time to decision |

## Selection rule

Choose the smallest motion that proves the intended outcome. Do not combine
multiple systems merely to make the demonstration look more complete.

## Required proof

1. Synthetic starting state
2. Visible evidence or grounding
3. Human decision point
4. Measurable outcome
5. Reset or fallback path
